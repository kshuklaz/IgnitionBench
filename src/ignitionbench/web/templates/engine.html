    Your preparation notes

    Characterization is live-fire testing. a and n come from measuring real burns — a strand burner or a small instrumented test motor — under a certified mentor or RSO at a legal test site, with remote ignition and the smallest samples that give data. IgnitionBench fits the numbers; the testing discipline is yours. Never scale up an uncharacterized batch.

    Test burns

    One row per burn: the pressure it burned at and the measured burn rate (sample length ÷ burn time). Spread points across the pressure range you plan to operate in — at least 3 burns for a fit.
